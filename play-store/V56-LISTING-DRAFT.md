## Full description

Plan your moves in Arrow Escape: Rescue Puzzle. Clear a maze of arrows, open a route to the golden key and rescue the character before danger reaches the cage.

Each arrow follows its path as it escapes. Look carefully at the whole route: another arrow or the moving arrow's own tail can block the way. Choose a safe move, then work through the puzzle until the golden key can escape last.

• Explore 1,000 levels across 25 chapters.
• Solve increasingly challenging boards with short, medium and long arrows.
• Rescue characters from water, heat and sand environments.
• Try a daily challenge and choose a booster after completing it.
• Use hints and other boosters when a puzzle needs a fresh perspective.
• Earn coins and stars, track your progress and revisit levels.

Play at your own pace, think ahead and find the escape route.
