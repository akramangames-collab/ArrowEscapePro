V16.0.3 makes progression more rewarding and replayable:
• Every level now earns a 1–3 star rating; 3★ requires no mistakes and no assists.
• A deterministic Daily Challenge gives everyone the same SUPER HARD puzzle each UTC day, with up to 150 coins.
• Every 5th level gets deeper dependency chains, fewer obvious exits and a +75 milestone bonus.
• Levels 25, 50, 100, 150 and 200 are Boss Milestones with an additional +75 bonus.
• Milestone intros, boss styling and saved best-star ratings make progression feel more special.
The approved snake/path-following arrow movement and production/test AdMob separation are preserved.
