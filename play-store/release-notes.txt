V16.0.4 introduces the new premium Arrow Escape experience:
• New dark navy / electric-blue home screen with clear Play, Levels, Daily Challenge, Rewards and Achievements navigation.
• Level select has premium dark cards, saved stars, SUPER HARD markers and Boss styling.
• Treasure chests now appear inside verified empty pockets of selected mazes. They never block arrows or affect collision/solvability.
• Clear the arrows around a chest to open it. Normal treasures award 25 coins, SUPER HARD treasures 50, and Boss treasures 100.
• Each campaign treasure can be rewarded only once, so replay/restart cannot be used to farm coins.
• New achievement screen tracks level progress, stars and treasure collection.
• Gameplay board stays clean and readable instead of copying the heavy 3D block style.
• Approved snake/path-following movement, 200 solvable levels, stars, Daily Challenge and AdMob separation are preserved.
