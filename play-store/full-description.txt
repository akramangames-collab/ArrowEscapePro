Find the way out, one arrow at a time.

Arrow Escape: Puzzle Maze is an offline-friendly arrow puzzle and logic game with 200 handcrafted-style maze levels, Daily Challenges, rewards, achievements, unlockable arrow designs and board themes.

SOLVE ARROW LOGIC PUZZLES
Study the maze, find an arrow with a completely clear escape path and tap to send it out. The arrowhead moves forward while its body follows the original bends like a snake. Arrows can block one another, and your own tail can block an escape too, so every move matters.

PLAN BEFORE YOU TAP
A blocked move costs a heart. Look ahead, clear arrows in the right order and open safe escape routes through increasingly dense puzzle layouts.

200 CHALLENGING LEVELS
Progress through 200 arrow mazes with growing difficulty, special mechanics, milestone puzzles and boss-style challenges. Each campaign puzzle is validated to have a solution.

DAILY CHALLENGE
Take on a separate super-hard Daily Challenge for a chance to earn 200 coins. Daily puzzles are separate from the 200-level campaign, giving you a fresh reason to return each day.

EARN COINS & BUILD YOUR STREAK
Complete puzzles, earn stars, collect level rewards, claim daily streak bonuses and watch optional rewarded ads for extra coins. Use coins for helpful gameplay options and cosmetic unlocks.

HINTS WHEN YOU NEED THEM
Each level starts with free hints. Additional hints and optional assists are available when you get stuck, while the same full-clearance rules remain in force.

CUSTOMIZE YOUR GAME
Unlock different arrow types, arrow colors and board themes. Cosmetic choices change the look and feel of the game without changing puzzle difficulty.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
Earn achievements, collect stars, clear milestone levels and build your daily streak while progressing through the full campaign.

BUILT-IN HOW TO PLAY
New players are guided through the core controls, and the tutorial can be opened again from Settings whenever needed.

PLAY OFFLINE
Core puzzle gameplay works offline. Internet access is only needed for advertising, rewarded-ad bonuses and consent-related services. No player account is required.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Your gameplay progress, coins and settings are stored on your device. Privacy controls and the privacy policy are available inside the app.

If you enjoy brain puzzles, logic games, maze puzzles, arrow games and order-based escape challenges, Arrow Escape: Puzzle Maze is designed for you.
