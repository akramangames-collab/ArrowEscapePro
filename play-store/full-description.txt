Find the way out, one arrow at a time.

Arrow Escape: Puzzle Maze is a logic puzzle with 200 challenging levels. Study each maze, spot an arrow with a clear path and tap to send it out. The arrowhead travels straight ahead while its body follows through every bend.

PLAN BEFORE YOU TAP
Arrows block one another. Clear them in a thoughtful order to open the maze. A blocked tap costs a heart, so look ahead before making your move.

200 HARD MAZES
Work through Hard, Expert and Master levels, with increasingly dense layouts and more arrows to untangle. Each puzzle has a way out.

EARN COINS AS YOU PLAY
Complete levels to earn coins, collect perfect-clear and milestone bonuses, and claim a daily reward. Spend your coins on helpful hints, an extra heart or a continue when you need one. Coins are virtual game currency and have no cash value.

HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
Each level attempt starts with two free hints. Additional hints cost 25 coins. Optional rewarded ads can earn coins or a selected erase or revive benefit.

PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
Your progress, coins and settings are saved on your device. Adjust sound, vibration and arrow contrast, or revisit the built-in guide whenever you like.

Core puzzles can be played offline. The game contains ads; ad rewards and advertising consent screens require an internet connection. No player account is required.
