Privacy Policy — Arrow Escape: Puzzle Maze
Effective date: 21 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to Arrow Escape: Puzzle Maze, Android package com.arrowescape.pro.

Local gameplay data
The game saves your current level, completed-level stars, coins, hints, hearts, saved boosters, reward receipts, streaks and preferences in the app's private Android storage. This data supports offline gameplay, restoring progress and preventing duplicate rewards. There is no developer-operated cloud save or user-account database for this data. Device backup settings may separately back up app data to your Android/Google account.

Google Play Games
Google Play Games sign-in is optional and handled by Google. If enabled, the game may display your Play Games profile name and status. We do not receive your Google account password, and signing in does not upload local game progress to a developer-operated server.

Advertising, device data and consent
The release version uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) and optional Google Play Games services. When permitted by the applicable consent/age-treatment settings, the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes and shares data with Google for ad delivery, analytics and fraud prevention. Google documents the SDK's automatic processing of:
- IP address, which may indicate approximate location;
- app and ad interactions, including app launches, taps and video views;
- diagnostic and performance information;
- device or account identifiers, including the app set ID and other applicable identifiers.

The game removes the Android AD_ID permission from its merged application manifest and marks ad requests as child-directed, with a G-rated ad-content maximum. The advertising SDK documentation describes Android advertising ID as a potential data type in general; our app does not request the advertising ID permission. Other identifiers may still be handled by Google's services. We do not request precise GPS location, photos, microphone, contacts or camera permissions.

The app asks Google's UMP whether ads may be requested before loading them. The AdMob configuration requests non-personalized, age-appropriate treatment for child users, and child-directed requests are designed to prevent interest-based advertising and remarketing. Some advertising, such as banner and interstitial ads, may be displayed without a reward. Rewarded ads are optional and used for boosters, revives or coins. Core puzzles can be played without watching rewarded ads. Data processed by Google's SDK is sent encrypted in transit using TLS.

Where legally required and available, you can review advertising choices from Settings > Privacy choices. Your device and Google account controls may offer further advertising privacy options. Internet access is needed to load advertising, consent forms and online platform services; offline puzzles still function without it.

Data retention and deletion
Local saved progress stays on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the game, subject to Android backups. You can delete this local data by clearing the app's storage. Data already processed by Google's services is subject to Google's retention and deletion policies; clearing local storage does not automatically remove it from Google.
