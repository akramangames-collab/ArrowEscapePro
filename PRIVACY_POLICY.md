# Privacy Policy — Arrow Escape: Puzzle Maze

**Effective date: 21 October 2026**

This policy covers Arrow Escape: Puzzle Maze (Android package `com.arrowescape.pro`), provided by **AK Software labs**. The developer repository is `akramangames-collab/ArrowEscapePro`.

## Game data on your device

The game saves your coin balance, level progress, hearts, hints, erasers, reward claims and settings in the app's private Android storage. These records let you resume playing and prevent duplicate rewards. The game has no player account system and no developer-operated server for synchronizing these records. Android may include app data in device backups when backup is enabled in your device or Google account settings.

## Advertising and consent

The release app includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. When ads are requested, Google's advertising SDK processes IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), ad/app interactions, diagnostic information and device or account identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID. Google uses and shares advertising-related data for ads, analytics and fraud prevention. App set ID has non-advertising uses such as analytics and fraud prevention. The game does not request GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.

Google's SDK encrypts its network traffic using TLS. Production ad requests wait until Google's consent system says ads may be requested. Where available, use **Settings > Privacy choices** to review your advertising choices. You can also reset or delete the advertising ID through Android's advertising settings. Rewarded videos are optional. Core puzzles can be played without watching a rewarded video; ads and online consent screens require a network connection.

## Children and family audiences

Arrow Escape is offered to children and adults. The game does not ask players to provide a name, email address, date of birth or account login. Local game progress is stored as described above; advertising SDK processing is described separately and must not be understood as "no data collected."

AdMob is configured with a maximum ad content rating of **G (General Audiences)** for this app, and sensitive ad categories are blocked. Child-directed ad treatment restricts interest-based advertising and remarketing and the use of advertising identifiers for children; the SDK data types listed above describe possible processing across app versions and eligible requests, not a claim that every identifier is sent on every request. We do not operate a developer account database for children. A parent or guardian may contact **akramangames@gmail.com** about a child's privacy without posting personal information to the public issue tracker.

## Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to Android backup and restore. Clearing storage removes your saved progress and coins from that installation. Manage any device backups separately in your Android or account settings. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or delete your local save. Data processed by Google is retained and managed according to Google's privacy policy and user controls. Clearing this game's storage does not delete data already processed by Google.
